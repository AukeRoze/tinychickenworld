# EP3 intro & outro — Gemini Pro pakket (still → foto-naar-video)

Twee stappen per clip, beide in de Gemini-app:
1. **Still** maken (beeld-modus): refs bijvoegen + de still-prompt → genereer het startframe.
2. **Foto-naar-video** (videogen/Omni): die still als startbeeld + de motion-prompt → clip van ~10s.

**Consistentie-regel (bij de STILL meeplakken):**
> Keep the characters EXACTLY as in the attached reference images. Pip: cream-white round chick, straw farmer hat + red neck bandana (no glasses). Mo: blue-grey anvil-shaped chick, tall red comb + red knitted scarf (no hat, no glasses). Bo: tan/sandy slim upright chick, round thin-framed glasses + green scarf + spiky cowlick (no hat). Soft 3D Pixar/Illumination style, never photorealistic, and NO text, letters or watermark anywhere.

> Audio-let-op: je pipeline dubt de stemmen apart (ElevenLabs). Gemini's video-modus kan zélf audio genereren — wil je je eigen dub gebruiken, zet Gemini's audio dan uit of negeer het. De motion-prompt noemt de timing/woorden zodat de snavelbeweging klopt, niet om Gemini te laten inspreken.

## INTRO

**Bijvoegen (still): Pip, Mo, Bo**

### Stap 1 — STILL-prompt
A calm, bright warm golden-hour farm world that matches the channel banner: soft rolling green hills, a friendly RED BARN and a WINDMILL in the background, a winding little stream, scattered sunflowers and daisies, and a big warm low SUN glowing softly in the sky. The three little chickens stand together in the lower centre, framed as a MEDIUM shot — fully visible from the waist up so their wings and round bodies are comfortably inside the frame with room to move — smiling warmly at the camera, with calm open meadow and sky around and above them so a title can be placed over the scene later. Calm, centred, uncluttered composition. No signboard, no sign, no banner and NO text anywhere.

### Stap 2 — MOTION-prompt (foto-naar-video op de intro-still)
Open on all THREE little cartoon chickens ALREADY standing together in the lower centre of the sunny meadow, fully visible from the very first frame. NONE of them appears, pops up, rises, walks in, hatches or fades in — all three are present and in frame the ENTIRE time. They greet the viewer ONE BY ONE in a BRISK, warm rhythm — three quick greeting turns packed into the FIRST FOUR SECONDS, each turn about ONE second, immediately one after another with NO long pauses between them. As EACH chicken greets it clearly performs its OWN signature quirk so the viewer instantly learns who is who: seconds 0.5-1.6 Pip gives a little wing-wave and her signature DOUBLE WINK — two quick winks in a row with the SAME right eye — her beak clearly opening and closing during HER second only; seconds 1.7-2.8 Mo does his signature move — a quick tug to snug his RED knitted scarf at the neck with one wing-tip (the scarf stays firmly on, never removed) — with a small nod, HIS beak moving during his second only; seconds 2.9-4.0 Bo does her signature move — pushing her ROUND glasses up the beak with a wing-tip — and a cheerful little bob, HER beak moving during her second only. From second 4 onward all three SETTLE, sit still together in the lower centre and smile warmly at the camera with beaks CLOSED, leaving calm open space above them. From second 4.0 until the very final frame, freeze all blinking: ALL THREE chickens' EYES MUST BE WIDE OPEN, bright and looking warmly at the camera, smiling — never closed, never mid-blink, never half-closed. (This eyes-open rule applies ONLY from second 4.0 on — Pip's double-wink in her 0.5-1.6s greeting is intended and MUST still happen.) NO signboard, no sign, no banner and NO text anywhere. STABILITY: Pip's straw hat stays FIRMLY on her head the entire time — it never flies off, lifts or falls. Each chicken STAYS in the exact same spot it starts in (same left/middle/right position as the start image) and NEVER swaps places, slides across or crosses past another. Calm, gentle, unhurried and warm — NOT busy, NOT high-energy, NO laughing. Only a soft breeze, a couple of drifting petals and 1-2 butterflies far in the background. BEAK / MOUTH: do NOT lip-sync or mouth exact words and do NOT hold a wide gaping beak; on its OWN greeting turn the speaking chicken clearly opens and closes its beak a few times (a simple visible 'talking' motion), while the other two keep beaks CLOSED and still — gentle rounded open-close, never a wide gape. Keep each chicken EXACTLY as designed: Pip cream-white, ROUND, straw hat + red bandana, no glasses; Mo blue-grey, ANVIL-shaped, tall red comb + red knitted scarf, no hat/glasses; Bo tan/sandy, slim UPRIGHT, round thin-framed glasses + green scarf, no hat. Never swap their accessories, never duplicate or add a chicken, no morphing. Soft 3D Pixar/Illumination look, never photorealistic.

## OUTRO

The SAME warm golden-hour farm world that matches the channel banner (soft rolling green hills, a friendly RED BARN and a WINDMILL, a winding little stream, scattered sunflowers and daisies), but the warm sun now sits a little LOWER and gently DIMMED for a soft, cosy end-of-day golden glow. EXACTLY THREE chickens in the whole picture — Pip, Mo and Bo, each appearing ONCE, no duplicates and no extra chickens. They sit CLOSE TOGETHER, LOW in the frame — all three fully inside the BOTTOM THIRD of the picture — smiling and giggling warmly at each other. The UPPER TWO THIRDS of the frame are deliberately CALM, uncluttered and EMPTY as clean cinematic NEGATIVE SPACE — just a smooth, soft warm evening-sky gradient and gently blurred farm bokeh, with NOTHING and NOBODY else there. No signboard, no sign, no banner and NO text anywhere. Wholesome, cosy composition.

### Stap 2 — MOTION-prompt (foto-naar-video op de outro-still)
STATIC, calm camera — locked off, or at most a very slow, minimal push-in. EXACTLY THREE cartoon chickens in the whole clip — Pip, Mo and Bo, each appearing ONCE; never duplicate a chick and never add extra chickens (not in the background, the bokeh or the empty sky). The three little chickens sit CLOSE TOGETHER, LOW in the frame — all three fully inside the BOTTOM THIRD of the picture — in warm golden-hour light. All three chicks look toward the camera with a shared, warm, giggling energy, leaning slightly into each other happily. While beaming warmly at the camera, Pip clearly opens and closes her beak to say cheerfully: "See you in the next adventure!". The UPPER TWO THIRDS of the frame stay deliberately CALM, uncluttered and EMPTY the whole clip as clean cinematic NEGATIVE SPACE — just a smooth, soft warm evening-sky gradient and gently blurred farm bokeh, with NOTHING appearing there (no birds, no butterflies, no objects). NO waving, NO held-up objects, NO signboard, no sign, no banner and NO text anywhere. Only a soft breeze low in the grass, warm DIMMED end-of-day light, cosy, gentle and unhurried. Keep each chicken EXACTLY as designed: Pip cream-white, ROUND, straw hat + red bandana, no glasses; Mo blue-grey, ANVIL-shaped, tall red comb + red knitted scarf, no hat/glasses; Bo tan/sandy, slim UPRIGHT, round thin-framed glasses + green scarf, no hat. Never swap accessories, never duplicate or add a chicken, no morphing. Soft 3D Pixar/Illumination look, never photorealistic.
