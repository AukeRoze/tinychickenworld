# Flow — klaarstaande EP3-prompts (volgende credit-dag)

Setup per clip in Google Flow (project Tiny Chicken World): create-box → **Agent UIT** → **Video** → **Ingredients** → de genoemde characters toevoegen (single-select per "Add to Prompt", chips stapelen) → landscape 16:9, 1x, **Omni Flash**, **10s** (=15 credits) → prompt plakken → **→**.

Prompts zijn al opgeschoond (mojibake weg, Pip = PURE WHITE, "Animate the characters"). Scène 24 heeft een harde anti-duplicatie-clausule erbij na de eerdere achtergrond-duplicatie.

**Prioriteit: eerst scène 24 her-genereren** (de andere 3 waren al goed).

---

## Scène 24 (RE-ROLL — payoff "Mama? Big sister.")

**Ingredients:** Pip, Mo, Bo, Duck

```
Animate the characters with slow, gentle, natural, child-friendly motion. Action: Intimate close-up of TWO characters only: the tiny lemon-yellow duckling with flat orange bill looking up with round trusting eyes at PIP (the cream-white chick wearing her straw farmer hat and red neck bandana - Pip MUST be the chick in this shot). Pip looks down at the duckling with solemn tenderness, one wing gently resting on the duckling's damp head-tuft. Warm golden dusk light halos both of them. Mo is only soft blurred shapes far in the background. A single firefly glows between them. Camera: dramatic low angle looking slightly up at the tiny chicks, the moment towering and grand, 85mm long lens, slow, deliberate cinematic push-in toward the emotional peak, lock focus on the main character's face at the heart of the moment, shallow, the background melting into soft bokeh around the peak. Setting: An enormous ancient oak on a low hill, its huge gnarled trunk wider than three chicks tall, sweeping branches forming a leafy green canopy, a braided-rope wooden swing on a low branch, a knot-hole that looks like a kind face, acorns scattered around its roots, a small carved sign on the trunk reading "MEET HERE!", soft warm dusk light, long shadows. Colour mood: rich saturated warm golden light - the brightest, most joyful palette with glowing highlights. Beat goal: Mama? Big sister. - the parent wink and the exhale beat. Performance: clearly convey tender (5/5). Telegraph the moment: a beat before the main action, show a big, clear anticipatory flash of tender - eyes widening, a sharp little intake, body tensing - then let the beat land. Character identity - match the character references EXACTLY (same design, colours and accessories), do not redesign anyone. Key cues: Pip: PURE WHITE (clean white, never cream or yellow), perfectly ROUND ball-of-fluff body, straw farmer hat + red neck bandana, HUGE sparkling brown eyes, the smallest chick of the trio. Mo: blue-grey, broad bottom-heavy ANVIL-shaped body, tall bright-red comb, red knitted neck scarf, steady warm-brown eyes, slightly the largest of the trio. Bo: tan/sandy-brown, slim UPRIGHT body with a spiky cowlick tuft on top, round thin-framed eyeglasses + green neck scarf, bright amber eyes, the thinnest chick and middle in height. Duckling is a bright lemon-yellow chick, ALWAYS wearing none - the only cast member without an accessory (being the baby IS the identity) (clearly visible, never dropped or swapped); silhouette: the tiniest of the four - a round yellow puffball with a FLAT WIDE BILL and one upright head-tuft; reads as 'baby duck' at a glance; feathers: ultra-soft lemon-yellow down, slightly damp spiky tuft on top in his first scenes, fluffy in later episodes; build: clearly the smallest of the group - about two-thirds of Bo's height, round baby proportions, oversized head, tiny webbed feet; weight: feather-light and wobbly - tips over when he flaps too enthusiastically; eyes: very dark warm brown, huge and glossy, with oversized baby catch-lights. Duckling must NEVER wear any hat, any scarf or bandana, any glasses, any pointed chicken beak, any red comb on the head. NEVER swap accessories between the chickens - each keeps ONLY its own hat/glasses/scarf and its own body colour; do not move a hat, glasses or scarf onto another chicken, and never give one chicken two accessories. Cast lock: this shot has EXACTLY 4 characters (Pip, Mo, Bo and Duckling) - these and no others. CRITICAL BACKGROUND RULE: there is EXACTLY ONE duckling in the entire frame and EXACTLY ONE of each chick - Pip, Mo and Bo. Mo and Bo appear ONCE each, small and softly blurred, far in the background. Absolutely NO other chick or duckling anywhere - no second duckling, no duplicate of any character, nothing extra near the swing, in the bokeh, behind the tree or at the edges of frame. No extra character, chicken, animal or silhouette appears or wanders into the background, and none of them is replaced or swapped out. They may move within the frame (and partly in or out of it as the action calls for), but the cast stays this exact set. Relative size: Pip is the smallest, Mo is slightly larger, Bo is slightly taller and slimmer, the duckling is the tiny baby, clearly smaller than any of the chicks. Their proportions never change between or within shots. The characters move with small lifelike motion - blinking, soft breathing, slight head and wing movement, with only small soft beak movements (do NOT lip-sync words - keep the beak mostly closed with subtle motion) - with soft ambient life (drifting petals or fireflies, gently swaying grass). Motion eases in and out with a gentle anticipation dip before the main action and a slow, soft settle after; weight follows through naturally - nothing moves at a robotic constant speed. Signature character motion: Pip leans in close with bright wide eyes when curious, one wingtip briefly touching the BRIM of her straw hat - the hat NEVER comes off her head, never in her wing, never on the ground. Mo gives his red knitted scarf a quick tug with one wingtip to snug it up at the neck (the scarf always stays on - never removed). Bo pushes her glasses up the beak with a wingtip before a joke. Duckling waddle-bounces twice on the spot and flaps both stubby wings when happy. Keep every character's identity, colours, proportions and signature accessories (straw hat, bandana, scarf, eyeglasses) perfectly stable across all frames - absolutely no morphing, no flicker, accessories never change. Smooth, steady camera. Soft 3D Pixar/Illumination cartoon look: fluffy down feathers with gentle subsurface shading, a small matte beak, extra-large glossy eyes with bright catch-lights, a warm rim-light defining each character's silhouette, smooth rounded storybook surfaces and shallow soft depth of field. Warm rich colours; never photorealistic, never flat 2D, no micro-texture.
```
